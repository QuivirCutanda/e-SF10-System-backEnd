--------------------------------------------------
## get-active-curriculum
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
             http://localhost:3001/esf10/curriculum/curriculum-subjects/:curriculum_id
