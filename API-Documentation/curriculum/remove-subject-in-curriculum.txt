--------------------------------------------------
## remove-subject-in-curriculum
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        DELETE
    -EndPoint
            http://localhost:3001/esf10/curriculum/remove-subject/:curriculum_id/:subject_id
--------------------------------------------------
