--------------------------------------------------
## get-Active-Curriculum-Subjects
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
             http://localhost:3001/esf10/curriculum//curriculum-subjects/active/1
            *note: 1 is constant it is represent as active
