--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
              http://localhost:3001/esf10/school-year/school-year/:school_year_id
