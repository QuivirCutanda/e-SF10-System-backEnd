--------------------------------------------------
## Search Subject
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
         http://localhost:3001/esf10/subjects/search-subjects?query=math
         or
         http://localhost:3001/esf10/subjects/search-subjects?query=math&grade_level=1
         or
         http://localhost:3001/esf10/subjects/search-subjects
--------------------------------------------------
