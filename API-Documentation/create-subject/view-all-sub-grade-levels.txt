--------------------------------------------------
## view-all-sub-grade-levels
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Content-Type
        Value:application/json
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
         http://localhost:3001/esf10/subjects/view-all-sub-grade-levels
--------------------------------------------------


*This endpoint is get all subject by grade level who is active curriculum
