--------------------------------------------------
## Update Student Info
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Authorization
        Value:Bearer paste your generated token here
    -Method
        PUT
    -EndPoint
        http://localhost:3001/esf10/students/<student-lrn>/update

    -Body:raw
--------------------------------------------------
Example requist
--------------------------------------------------
{
    "first_name": "John",
