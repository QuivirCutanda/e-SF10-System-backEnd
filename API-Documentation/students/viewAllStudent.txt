--------------------------------------------------
## View all Student
--------------------------------------------------

#Prerequisites
    -Autorization
        No Auth
    -Headers (add)
        Key:Authorization
        Value:Bearer paste your generated token here
    -Method
        GET
    -EndPoint
       http://localhost:3001/esf10/students/all

        *To go to the next page (page 2), append ?page=2 to the URL
        http://localhost:3001/esf10/students/all?page=2

        *If you want to explicitly specify the limit:
        http://localhost:3001/esf10/students/all?page=1&limit=12
